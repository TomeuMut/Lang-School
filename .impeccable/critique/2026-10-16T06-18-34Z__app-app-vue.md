## Strengths

## Priority issues and applied changes

1. P1: changing scope or starting another skill could discard an unfinished session, even when selecting the active language. Added same-scope no-op and inline confirmation before replacing an active session; navigation between views preserves it. Confirmation receives focus.
2. P2: resume and restart had inconsistent behavior. Home now offers Continue session with exercise position; practice navigation resumes only unfinished sessions.
3. P2: large motivational framing competed with the exercise, especially on mobile. Practice gets a compact task heading, less chrome and reduced spacing; the question receives focus after advancing.
4. P2: disabled save action did not explain its prerequisites. Added visible contextual hints, checklist instructions, explicit draft-save notice and browser leave-page protection where supported.
5. P2: history required remembering the original question. Expanded review includes prompt, passage, student's response, correct answer and explanation.

Additional fixes: accessible and visible mobile level label; dark foreground on the pale word-card illustration; explicit incorrect-answer text; session progress measures completed answers rather than current position; readable textarea and supporting copy.
